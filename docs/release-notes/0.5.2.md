A change for Google Play only: StudioSnap is now offered there to Googlebooks and other desktop-class Android devices, and no longer to phones and tablets. Everything still runs on your Googlebook: StudioSnap has no INTERNET permission.

**Download:** grab `StudioSnap.apk` below. It installs over 0.5.1 and keeps your settings. If you installed 0.4 or earlier from GitHub before 30 September 2026, uninstall it once first: releases are signed with a new key, the same one Google Play uses.

## Changes

- **Google Play is desktop-only now.** StudioSnap is built for the Googlebook desktop, but Play offered it to more than 5,000 phone and tablet models too. The app now says it needs a PC-type device, which Googlebooks are.
- Nothing changes in the app itself, and installs from GitHub are not affected.
