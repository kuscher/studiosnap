A fix for Google Play: StudioSnap now shows up as compatible with Googlebooks that only have a front camera. Everything still runs on your Googlebook: StudioSnap has no INTERNET permission.

**Download:** grab `StudioSnap.apk` below. It installs over 0.5.0 and keeps your settings. If you installed 0.4 or earlier from GitHub before 30 September 2026, uninstall it once first: releases are signed with a new key, the same one Google Play uses.

## Changes

- **Available on every Googlebook in Google Play.** Play listed StudioSnap as "not compatible" with Googlebooks that have a front webcam and no rear camera, such as the Dell XPS Googlebook and the HP Googlebook 14. The app asked for the camera permission without saying the camera is optional, so Play assumed it needs a rear camera. The camera and the microphone are now declared optional.
- Nothing changes in the app itself. Installs from GitHub were never affected.
