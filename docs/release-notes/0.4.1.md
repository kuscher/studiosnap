A small update ahead of Google Play: StudioSnap now says plainly what its accessibility service does before you turn it on, and the recording notification shows up. Everything still runs on your Googlebook: StudioSnap has no INTERNET permission.

**Download:** grab `StudioSnap.apk` below. It installs over 0.4 and keeps your settings. If you installed 0.4 or earlier from GitHub before 30 September 2026, uninstall it once first: releases are now signed with a new key, the same one Google Play uses.

## Changes

- **Clearer setup.** The one-time setup explains what the accessibility service lets StudioSnap do: check key presses only for the Screenshot key or Action + Shift + S (other keys pass straight through and are never recorded), take the screenshot, and, while you capture, read where windows and on-screen elements are, read their text for Text, and scroll a window for Scroll capture. Its button is now **Agree and turn on**.
- **The home screen's Turn on StudioSnap** shows that explanation again instead of going straight to Android's settings.
- **The recording notification shows.** Before your first recording, StudioSnap asks once to send notifications, so "StudioSnap is recording" and its **Stop** button appear in the notification shade. Without them Android hides the notification (you can still stop from the recording pill, or under Active apps in Quick Settings).

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** asked once, before the first recording. Nothing else changes.
