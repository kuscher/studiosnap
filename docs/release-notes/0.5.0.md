A clearer first run, a bar that knows you're recording, and messages that always show. Everything still runs on your Googlebook: StudioSnap has no INTERNET permission.

**Download:** grab `StudioSnap.apk` below. It installs over 0.4.1 and keeps your settings. If you installed 0.4 or earlier from GitHub before 30 September 2026, uninstall it once first: releases are signed with a new key, the same one Google Play uses.

All five changes come from Jesse's testing of 0.4 on an Acer Googlebook 14 (#8 to #12).

## New in 0.5.0

- **First run on one page.** The four features in a grid and one setup card: the accessibility service, notifications, and a line on the microphone and camera. **Start capturing** is always there and unlocks once the service is on.
- **Turn on explains itself every time.** Every **Turn on** (first run, home, Settings › Permissions) opens a short dialog that says what the service lets StudioSnap do and that what it reads stays on your Googlebook. **Agree and turn on** opens Android's settings; **Cancel** does nothing.
- **Permissions in Settings.** Settings has a Permissions section for the service and notifications, and home shows a card while notifications are off.
- **Video mode.** The bar's Record mode is now called **Video** (modes are nouns, buttons are verbs). Open the bar during a recording and it starts in Video mode with a red **Stop** button; a stray click elsewhere only closes the bar, never the recording.

## Fixes

- **Messages always show.** Hints like "Already recording" or "Stop the recording to change the mic or system audio" were dropped by Android when notifications were off. They now show as a small pill next to the bar.
- **Camera bubble controls stay out of recordings.** While recording, the bubble's controls come up on a click, not on hover, and fold away after 3 seconds.
- **The camera turns off at Stop**, and the recording is saved off the main thread, so StudioSnap stays responsive while a long recording is copied to Movies.

## Permissions

- No changes. Notifications can now also be allowed from the setup card or Settings; the microphone and camera are still asked for only when you first turn them on.
